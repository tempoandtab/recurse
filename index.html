Recurse

A puzzle game about boxes within boxes.

  • Push every box onto a glowing goal — ←↑↓→, WASD, or swipe.
  • Teal boxes are hollow. Walk into one through its gold door to explore the world inside.
  • Push boxes into and out of hollow boxes through their doors.
  • ∞ boxes are recursive. The world inside is the world you're in. Walk in through the gold door to go one level deeper — as deep as you like. Bump the back of the box to climb back out. Only boxes in the outer world count toward goals.
  • ↩ undoes freely, ⟲ restarts. Experiment!
